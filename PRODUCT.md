## Users

Private homeowners and cabin owners across Vestfold who want to do a property project themselves (drainage, deck, lawn, exterior painting, retaining wall, digging, tree work). They search for how to do the job, often before they know what equipment they need. They value quality and service over the lowest price, may want practical guidance or a little hands-on help, and like having a local partner over time.

## Product Purpose

BoligButler makes it simpler and safer to succeed with projects on the customer's own property. Equipment rental (scaffolding, mini digger, dumper, plate compactor, trailers, lift, chipper, tiller, laser) is a means, not the product. Success: the customer finishes the project well and safely, and comes back for the next one.

## Positioning

A local project partner, not a machine-rental counter. Equipment arrives with a "butler": delivery, on-site introduction, a project kit with the small things people always lack, laminated checklists ("Før du starter", "Før du leverer tilbake"), and an experienced adviser available by phone or on site. No packages (owner declined tiers, 2026-10-03): one offer, with delivery, introduction and project kit always included, and extra help (befaring, plan, on-site help, practical assistance, ordering masses) added on request.

## Operating Context

Customers start from a project ("Jeg skal drenere huset"), not a machine. Vestfold conditions: clay soils, frost/tele, wet autumns. Service area: all of Vestfold (confirmed by owner); earlier documents name Horten, Holmestrand and Tønsberg (incl. former Re).
